# JournalGuard evaluation protocol (draft v1)
Status: **not executed**. The target sample size of 150 is a study design, not a completed benchmark.

## Research question
How accurately does JournalGuard flag independently documented journal identity/provenance discrepancies without wrongly accusing legitimate journals?

## Sampling
Stratify by discipline, publisher type, geography and known verification difficulty. Include legitimate, documented deceptive and indeterminate cases. Do not use tool output to construct labels. Archive sources with retrieval dates. Two independent adjudicators plus a tie-break reviewer. Separate a held-out evaluation set from development cases.

## Outputs
Capture raw evidence, source URL, retrieval time, ISSN, publisher, DOI, registry outcome, tool version, classification, and explicit abstentions. Scopus journal coverage and individual article indexing must be evaluated separately with authorized evidence.

## Measures
Per-class precision and recall, false-positive rate, coverage, abstention rate, confusion matrix and confidence intervals. Provide subgroup error rates and document all exclusions. Do not report a percentage before the test runs.

## Responsible publication
Use careful language, preserve correction and appeal channels, avoid unsubstantiated predatory labels, and never equate missing indexing with fraud. Publish a reproducible methodology and dated results.
